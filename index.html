Our story

A tradition of making
medicines the careful way.

Michael McLoughlin has spent nearly 50 years preparing natural remedies by hand, in a tradition his family has carried across generations. Not in a laboratory removed from patients, but behind a counter, in a community, learning what natural ingredients could actually do for skin and wound recovery.

That craft knowledge — which combinations worked, which ingredients complemented each other, how a formulation behaved in real conditions — is the foundation of every Epitheal product. It never disappeared from pharmacy. It just never had the infrastructure to reach the people who needed it.

Epitheal was founded in the Connemara Gaeltacht to change that. A natural wound care range for people and animals, developed and validated first in veterinary practice and carried through to human applications. An alternative to antibiotics and steroids that works better and carries less risk — not because of a lab breakthrough, but because of a lifetime's knowledge, finally given the platform it deserves.
